Questions people ask before importing

Short answers, no sales voice.

Do I need n8n Cloud to use these workflows?

No. Every workflow runs on n8n self-hosted (free) and on n8n Cloud, version 1.40 or newer. You only need accounts for the services each workflow touches: Google Sheets, Telegram and email in the examples.

Do I have to write code?

No. Code nodes ship already written and tested. You edit message text and credentials. Each workflow carries sticky notes inside n8n explaining every step, plus a written guide.

Where does my data go?

It stays in your own n8n instance with your own credentials. Nothing reaches our servers. This site runs no analytics script and sets no cookies, which is also why there is no cookie banner.

Is the vendor bank-change guard really free?

Yes. The 60-node workflow, its documentation and the logic tests are MIT licensed and public. There is no paid tier of it. The paid pack is a different product, in Spanish, for accountancy practices.

Which language are the workflows in?

The clinic and finance workflows are fully in English: node names, sticky notes, emails, error messages. The four sector workflows for Spanish-speaking practices are in Spanish, and so is the paid pack.

Can I use these with clients and charge for it?

The MIT licence lets you use and modify the free workflows commercially, including inside client projects. You may not resell the files as templates. The paid pack carries its own commercial licence.

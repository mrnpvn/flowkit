How to read the result

If the monthly saving is smaller than the time it takes you to build and maintain a workflow, do not build it. Automation pays off when the task repeats every week and the rules are clear enough to write down.

Questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript. There is no form, no cookie and no request to any server.

What automation percentage should I use?

Between 40% and 60% is a reasonable starting point for reminding, copying and follow-up. The judgement call, the exception and the client relationship stay human.

Is the saving real money in the bank?

Not necessarily. The calculation prices time at an hourly cost. It turns into money if you invoice more, serve clients better, or avoid a hire.
